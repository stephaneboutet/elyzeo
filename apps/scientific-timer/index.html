Scientific Timer

The productivity app for researchers. Break complex lab protocols into timer-tasks and run as many experiments in parallel as you need.

Download on theApp Store

Free · iPhone · iOS 18 or later

Built for the bench

Designed for molecular and cellular biologists who juggle incubations, spins and washes across several experiments.

Protocols, step by step

Improve your productivity

Every protocol becomes a clear sequence of timer-tasks, numbered in order, so you always know what's next and never lose your place.

Parallel timers

Work more efficiently

Run as many timer-tasks in parallel as you need, across all your experiments. Each one shows how much time is left at a glance.

Notifications

Work in a quieter lab

Instead of a room full of beeping timers, you get a subtle notification on your phone when each timer-task is done.

Search

Powerful search

Find the timer-tasks and protocols you need in seconds, even mid-experiment.

Curated library

Save time

Start from curated timer-tasks for well-known protocols from 10x Genomics, Qiagen, NEB and more, or build your own.

🔒

Your data stays on your device

Scientific Timer doesn't collect any data. There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your protocols and timers never leave your iPhone.
