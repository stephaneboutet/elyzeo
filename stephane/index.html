What I do

Where biology, technology and product meet

My career started at the bench, studying muscle stem cells at Stanford. It has since moved toward building the platforms and teams that let other scientists do their best work.

Build & scale R&D teams

I've grown and led organizations of up to 13 people across three functions, managed budgets of $2–5M, and run more than 30 external collaborations with academic and industry partners.

Take science to product

I've owned products end to end: prototyping, specs, manufacturing, validation and launch. That includes 6 single-cell launches at 10x Genomics and the REM-I reagent kit at Deepcell.

Bridge biology and AI

I've built ground truth, training data and biological validation for more than 8 AI models, from a cell-morphology foundation model to cancer classifiers for liquid biopsy.
